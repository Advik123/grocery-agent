Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Groceries-Agent is a personal grocery assistant that receives WhatsApp messages and helps add grocery items to OurGroceries shopping lists. This policy explains what information the app receives and how it is used.

Information We Receive

When you message Groceries-Agent on WhatsApp, the app may receive your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, message text, grocery item names, quantities, and the store or list name included in your message.

How We Use Information

The app uses message content only to understand your grocery request, identify the target store or list, add the requested items to OurGroceries, and send a confirmation or error response back through WhatsApp.

Third-Party Services

Groceries-Agent uses the WhatsApp Cloud API to receive and send WhatsApp messages. It may use Google Gemini to parse natural language grocery requests. It also uses OurGroceries to update shopping lists. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Storage

The app is designed for personal use and does not use a database. It keeps short-lived in-memory session state, such as the last store used or items waiting for store confirmation, so it can complete the current conversation. This session data is temporary and is cleared when the server process restarts.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the services needed to operate the app, such as WhatsApp, Google Gemini, and OurGroceries.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of any information associated with your WhatsApp number by following the instructions on the Data Deletion Instructions page.
