Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Groceries-Agent is a personal WhatsApp grocery assistant. The app does not use a persistent database, but you can still request deletion of any information associated with your WhatsApp number.

How To Request Deletion

Send an email to sinhaarti06@gmail.com with the subject line "Groceries-Agent Data Deletion Request".

Please include:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, including country code.
  • A short statement asking us to delete your Groceries-Agent data.

What Will Be Deleted

We will delete any temporary session state associated with your WhatsApp number, such as remembered store context or grocery items waiting for confirmation, if it is still present on the running server.

Data Not Stored By This App

Groceries-Agent does not maintain a database of message history, shopping history, or user profiles. WhatsApp, Google Gemini, and OurGroceries may retain information according to their own policies. To delete data held by those services, please use their respective account or privacy tools.

Processing Time

Deletion requests will be reviewed and completed within 30 days. If no persistent data exists for your WhatsApp number, we will reply to confirm that there is no app-stored data to delete.

Privacy Policy

You can review the Groceries-Agent privacy policy at /privacy.
